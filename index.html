02The studio

One pair of hands, from sketch to master.

Dracortia was born from a straightforward notion — sharing a passion and a skill set with a broader audience.

Across my professional work I've cultivated composition and programming side by side, and the studio is where those two meet. Composition that knows what an engine can do; implementation that respects the music.

A current role as Technical Audio Designer in the games industry — and past work as an Audio Programmer — means I build for the pipeline, not just for the demo. Game-ready assets, workflows that hold up, and an ear for what the player actually hears.
